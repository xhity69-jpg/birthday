My Devyashree,

Two years ago, I didn't know that one conversation could turn into a habit, and a habit into the softest part of my life. Somewhere between the ordinary messages and the calls that always ran too late, you became the person I tell everything to.

Thank you for two years of being exactly who you are — for laughing at your own jokes before you finish them, for the kindness you hand out so easily, for the way you make an ordinary Tuesday feel like something worth remembering.

I love the quiet version of us too: the unglamorous days, the comfortable silences, the small "did you eat?" messages. That's the part nobody else sees, and it's my favourite part.

I know I'm not perfect. But I hope you always feel how loved you are — on the loud days and the tired ones, on your best days and the days you don't quite feel like yourself. I'm not going anywhere.

Today the world celebrates the day you arrived in it. I get to celebrate that, and the fact that out of everyone, you chose to spend your days with me. Two years in, that still feels like the luckiest thing that has ever happened to me.

Happy birthday, my love. Here's to a year that's gentle with you — and to every birthday after this one, where I get to be the one writing you something like this.

I love you. Today, and in all the ordinary days after.

Always, Raktim ♡
